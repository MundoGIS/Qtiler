Story Sections

Sections are the building blocks of the page, rendered top to bottom. Hero: big banner with title, subtitle and optional call-to-action. Text: rich text with optional image beside or above it. Maps: gallery of published maps (cards, embedded or links). Cards: grid of small info cards with title, text and link. Social: row of links with icons. Each section can have its own visibility rule.
